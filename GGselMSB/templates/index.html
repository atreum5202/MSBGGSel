📐 Формула расчёта sell_price
① total_fee = category_fee + payment_fee + withdrawal_fee
② total_costs = source_price × (1 + total_fee + tax + risk_reserve) + fixed_costs
③ sell_price = total_costs ÷ (1 − target_margin), округление до {{ econ_round_label }}
④ profit = sell_price − total_costs; margin = profit ÷ sell_price
Если profit < min_net_profit ({{ econ.min_net_profit_rub }} ₽) → товар помечается как нерентабельный.
